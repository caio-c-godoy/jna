Home Improvement Estimate

Professional labor and materials estimate for stair preparation, flooring installation, interior painting, cabinet painting, and cabinet repairs.

Estimated Total Investment $13,395

Prepared by JNA Contractors LLC. Final schedule depends on material availability and site readiness.

Scope of Work

Labor + Selected Materials

Stair Preparation

$1,090
  • Remove carpet and existing flooring from stairs.
  • Prepare subfloor to receive new stair steps.
  • Sand all stair surfaces as needed.
  • Clean and prepare surfaces for glue installation.

Stair Installation

$2,345
  • Install 26 stair steps with white risers.
  • Client must purchase desired flooring material and preferred color.
  • Flooring material must be available on-site prior to installation.
  • Pricing includes labor only.
  • Painting, staining, and installation of natural wood are not included.

Landing Area Flooring Installation

$550
  • Labor for flooring installation on landing area.

Interior Painting

$3,680
  • Paint all brown walls in the family room, dining room, and kitchen area white.
  • Includes labor and materials.

Baseboards & Stair Risers Painting

$990
  • Paint all downstairs baseboards and stair risers.
  • Doors are not included.
  • Includes labor and materials.

Kitchen Cabinet Painting

$3,890
  • Paint kitchen cabinets in a similar brown color.
  • Includes labor and materials.

Kitchen Cabinet Repairs

$850
  • Repair kitchen cabinet moldings, kick plates, and sink panel.
  • Includes labor and materials.

Recommendation

We recommend purchasing flooring materials from DURALAST FLOORS USA, where we can assist with quantities and ensure high-quality materials with warranty support for the client.
